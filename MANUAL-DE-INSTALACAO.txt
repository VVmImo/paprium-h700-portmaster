PAPRIUM H700 — muOS INSTALLATION GUIDE — alpha 0.5.2

English documentation and installer-language revision of 0.5.1. Audio
behavior is unchanged. Experimental integrated emulation, not a native
game port or RetroArch core. User-tested on RG40XX H, H700 ARM64, 1 GB,
muOS 2601.0. Full-game and two-player testing remain incomplete.
No game ROM, music or personal saves are included.

1. CHOOSE THE DOWNLOAD
Archive Manager: Paprium-H700-muOS-alpha-0.5.2.muxupd
Manual extraction: paprium-h700-alpha-0.5.2.zip
Do not use the KNULLI package on muOS. Do not rename ZIP to .muxupd:
the internal layouts are different.

2. REQUIREMENTS AND CARD PATHS
Install/update PortMaster for your firmware. Requires ARM64, firmware SDL2,
libc/libm/libdl and GLIBC symbols up to 2.29. No RetroArch core, FFmpeg or
Python installation is needed on the device.

The .muxupd targets /mnt/union/ROMS/Ports, muOS's merged content path.
It does not require SD2. Physical paths are:
  SD1: /mnt/mmc/ROMS/Ports/
  SD2: /mnt/sdcard/ROMS/Ports/
The card holding the installer does NOT select its destination: unionfs
write rules decide the physical card. Use manual extraction to choose a
card explicitly. Avoid duplicate Paprium installations on both cards.
The launcher resolves the physical location, checking SD2 then SD1, as in
the observed PortMaster integration. Music is read from the physical path
because a streaming-read stall was previously observed through unionfs.
Complete installation/launch testing on both cards is still needed.

Allow at least 3 GB free AFTER copying ROM and MP3 files for the reference
soundtrack. Generated WAV files occupy about 2.24 GB; other track lengths
may require more. Keep sufficient battery or external power for conversion.

3. ARCHIVE MANAGER INSTALLATION
Card reader:
1) Shut down normally, remove the card and connect it to your PC.
2) Copy the .muxupd into ARCHIVE at the card root, for example:
   X:\ARCHIVE\Paprium-H700-muOS-alpha-0.5.2.muxupd
   X: means the actual card drive letter, not a fixed drive.
3) Safely eject, put the card back and power on.

SFTP alternatives, using your device IP and configured credentials:
  /mnt/mmc/ARCHIVE/Paprium-H700-muOS-alpha-0.5.2.muxupd  (SD1)
  /mnt/sdcard/ARCHIVE/Paprium-H700-muOS-alpha-0.5.2.muxupd (SD2)
Wait for the transfer to finish.

Open Applications → Archive Manager, select the 0.5.2 archive and install.
Wait for completion before returning to the menu. Do not manually extract
the .muxupd at the card root: Archive Manager handles its destination.

4. MANUAL INSTALLATION TO A SPECIFIC CARD
Extract paprium-h700-alpha-0.5.2.zip directly into ROMS/Ports on your chosen
card. These must be side by side:
  X:\ROMS\Ports\Paprium.sh
  X:\ROMS\Ports\paprium\paprium.aarch64
Do not leave an extra version-named directory around them. This method
does not also need the .muxupd. Do not copy the development workspace.

5. EXACT GAME-DATA DESTINATIONS
Find the installed paprium folder containing paprium.aarch64. Supply every
file to THAT installation; do not split executables and data across cards.
Create gamedata and gamedata/paprium if missing.

Uncompressed ROM, named exactly paprium.bin:
  PC:   X:\ROMS\Ports\paprium\gamedata\paprium.bin
  SFTP: <PORTS>/paprium/gamedata/paprium.bin
All 52 correctly named MP3 files, directly inside:
  PC:   X:\ROMS\Ports\paprium\gamedata\paprium\
  SFTP: <PORTS>/paprium/gamedata/paprium/

<PORTS> means /mnt/mmc/ROMS/Ports (SD1) or /mnt/sdcard/ROMS/Ports (SD2),
according to the physical installation. X: is that same card.
Example on SD2:
/mnt/sdcard/ROMS/Ports/paprium/gamedata/paprium/01 Theme of Paprium.mp3

Preserve spaces, numbers and punctuation from the appendix. Copy the MP3
files themselves, without adding another nested music folder. Watch for
hidden extensions producing paprium.bin.bin or .mp3.mp3. Do not leave the
ROM compressed or put game data in ARCHIVE. A separate paprium.wav is not
required and does not replace the 52 named tracks. Matching valid WAV files
may be supplied instead of MP3 files.

6. REFERENCE DATA AND COMPATIBILITY
Reference ROM used during development:
  Name: paprium.bin
  Exact size: 8,388,608 bytes (8 MiB)
  Product identifier: T-574120-00
  SHA-256:
  f9192ac55b915ac65765688bd4944c98d4ebe2ea994e6d312e2e6e52f7e79d09
A different hash identifies different data, not automatic incompatibility;
that revision needs testing. Renaming or changing another ROM's header
does not establish compatibility. In PowerShell, replace X: appropriately:
  (Get-Item -LiteralPath 'X:\ROMS\Ports\paprium\gamedata\paprium.bin').Length
  Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 -LiteralPath 'X:\ROMS\Ports\paprium\gamedata\paprium.bin'

TRACKS.txt and music-tracks.txt list all expected names. The converter
produces PCM16 stereo WAV at 48 kHz. The core accepts mono/stereo WAV at
8–48 kHz; prefer 48 kHz. Names and format do not prove the music content.

7. FIRST LAUNCH AND AUTOMATIC CONVERSION
In Ports, select the FILE Paprium.sh, possibly displayed as Paprium with
extensions hidden. Selecting the paprium folder only opens that folder.

Preparation checks all 52 tracks first. Valid existing WAV files are
reused. Each missing WAV needs an MP3 with the same base name. If both are
missing, preparation stops and identifies the track before converting.
With 52 MP3 files and no WAV files, expect:
  Converting music 1/52: ...
Keep power connected and wait. Duration depends on the card and files.
The game starts automatically afterward; no Internet, FFmpeg or Python
is required for conversion.

MP3 files are retained. WAV files are written beside them. Later launches
check and reuse completed WAV files. If preparation fails, correct the
cause and retry; valid completed WAV files are preserved. An invalid WAV
is not overwritten: move it outside the music directory, provide its MP3
and relaunch to regenerate that track. Updating to 0.5.2 does not require
converting existing valid music again.

8. INITIAL MINIGAME AND CONTROLS
If the initial minigame appears, hold Select and tap R1 once it is playable
to request a soft reset. If it does not progress, preserve the log.
Running the minigame alone does not prove full-game compatibility.

Physical input                    Mega Drive / function
D-pad / left stick                Directions
Left / bottom / right / top face   A / B / C / X
L1 / R1                           Y / Z
Start / Select                    Start / Mode
Select + Start                    Exit normally to muOS
Select + R1                       Soft reset; not the normal exit command

Physical positions avoid confusion between printed button labels.
Two controllers are supported in code but need physical testing.
Diagnostic keyboard: arrows, A/S/D, Z/X/C, Enter, right Shift; Escape exits,
F1 requests soft reset.

9. SAVES, UPDATING AND CLEAN TESTS
Save: <PORTS>/paprium/saves/paprium.srm, exactly 65,536 bytes.
Changed SRAM is checked every five seconds and on normal exit. Only
progress saved by the game is retained; there are no arbitrary-moment
savestates. Close the game before copying/restoring SRAM. Existing SRAM
with an invalid size is rejected without overwriting it.

To update, close the game and install over the same location. Public
packages contain no game data or saves to overwrite. An SRAM copy before
updating is optional.
For a genuinely clean test, close the game and remove ONLY Paprium.sh and
the installed paprium folder. This removes that installation's ROM, music,
WAV files, configuration and saves. Do not delete the whole Ports folder.
Reinstall, supply ROM and 52 MP3 files, and check conversion, launch,
controls, audio, normal exit and a second launch without reconversion.

10. AUDIO AND TROUBLESHOOTING
Audio behavior is unchanged from 0.5.1: 5 Hz DC rejection, interpolated ROM
PCM effects, about 50 ms of initial queue reserve, corrected per-voice gain
and a 6 kHz effects/echo reconstruction filter. Music bypasses that effects
filter. Original 4/8-bit effects still limit fidelity. User-confirmed audio
improvement does not prove exact reproduction throughout the game.
Comparison options: PAPRIUM_SFX_FILTER=0 bypasses the effects filter;
PAPRIUM_LEGACY_AUDIO_FILTER=1 enables the old global filter, off by default.
Normal installation/play does not require editing the launcher.

Folder opens: select the Paprium.sh file instead.
No Paprium entry: verify installation and physical paths.
Missing control.txt: install/update PortMaster for muOS.
Missing paprium.bin: check section 5, including hidden extensions.
Missing music: supply the exact named MP3 or valid matching WAV.
Conversion failed: check free space, MP3 integrity and logs.
Invalid WAV: preserve it outside the folder and regenerate from its MP3.
Freeze: try Select + Start; recover through the muOS menu if responsive.
After recovery, preserve the log before relaunching. Do not assume all
freezes have the same cause as the earlier unionfs streaming-read stall.

Logs:
  <PORTS>/paprium/log.txt
  <PORTS>/paprium/conf/conversion-status.txt
"Paprium physical data path" should show physical SD1 or SD2 storage.
Reports should include model, firmware, package version, ROM size/hash,
track/scene and error. Do not send ROM, music, passwords or personal saves.
A new launch replaces the previous game log.
